THE PURPOSE

A SPACE FOR VOICES

Where ideas become stories.

This literary magazine serves as a creative and academic space where different forms of writing come together. Through our original works, reflections, and literary analysis, we explore the ways stories can express ideas, values, experiences, and perspectives.

More than simply presenting our outputs, this collection represents our voices as students and creators --- showing how literature can connect imagination with real-life experiences and meaningful conversations.
